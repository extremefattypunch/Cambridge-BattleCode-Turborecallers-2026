# Experiment: q24_combo, LOOP.md §4 promotion gate (2026-11-01, cluster)

STRATEGIES **Q24**: the combined candidate. This report covers the full promotion gate (a-d) against the champion `tb_merge_b` and its mandatory analysis session. The bot was frozen for the gate, and no code was changed.

**Gate result: PASS on all four parts.**
- (a) 87/140, with a 95% Wilson lower bound of 0.539, and 0 botFaults.
- (b) Gauntlet 174/200 against the champion's 158/200. The worst per-opponent change is −1.
- (c) The release game was clean. fuelExhausted was 8.0 per game against 67.9.
- (d) All four mechanisms changed, and the gain survives removing the tiebreak maps.

## Hypothesis
- **What:** tb_merge_b plus four batch-1 patches that touch different subsystems. Each patch scored 51-60% alone over the 140 games:
  1. **Lock-memory fix** (h_3pick, `LOCK_MEMORY_FIX`). `pick_altar` skips altars *remembered* as enemy-locked, which ends the 2-cycle that froze gem carriers.
  2. **Gold-tiebreak play** (h_goldtie v2).
     - `no_tribute` is detected statically and switches the bot to gold valuation, Treasure emblems first, no banking and no attacks on guards.
     - A `stalled` mode (r300+, 0-0) turns on the respawn-rule fix and chip mode.
  3. **Group-balance fight rule all game** (h_earlyfight_all, v3). Each local fight is scored as (Σhp × Σdps) ours over theirs. At ≥ 1.4 the group commits; at ≤ 0.75 it regroups toward allies while hitting.
  4. **Fuel caching** (h_fightfuel). Bitboard `observe`, awake-only `threats` with a per-turn enemy cache, `retreat_fast` and `kit_units_fast`.
  - **Integration (new):** `TRIBUTE_USABLE_ONLY`. It is a no-op on all 70 local maps. Merge details are in `bots/q24_combo/NOTES.md`.
- **Expected effect:** the four gains should mostly add up, because they live in different subsystems. The single-patch totals were +5, +4, +14 and +2 over the 70/140 mirror baseline.
- **Source:** batch-1 reports `2026-11-01-h_3pick.md`, `-h_goldtie.md`, `-h_earlyfight_all.md`, `-h_fightfuel.md` and `2026-10-31-batch1-interim.md`.

## New hypotheses generated
- **Q29: side-B opening skirmish on fight maps.**
  - Evidence: 14 of q24's 20 blowout losses are as team B, with 48 vs 23 deaths before r300. 01hooks B had 3 deaths to camp#5 at (18,18) and 1 to camp#2 by r239; 00gem-fields B had 7 vs 1 early deaths; 00vault B lost two 114-115-gem carriers by r175. q24 won the A side of 9 of these 14 maps, most by a landslide.
  - Plan: trace the r<300 fights with `cambc run` stdout (group_balance ratio, commit/regroup and camp threat) on 01hooks B and 00gem-fields B.
  - Why: each converted map swings a 1,000-gem landslide. 4-5 games would add 3%.
- **Q30: large-map farming tempo.**
  - Evidence: on maps of ≥ 8,000 tiles q24 went 2/12. 02tangle had 33 vs 65 and 27 vs 53 clears with ≤ 2 kills, and deaths to camp#46/#47; the same collapse appears in 3 of 4 single-patch bots but not in the mirror. adgato-gemcombat5 B had 9 vs 26 clears. 01big-world A had mean tribute 17.4 vs 24.5.
  - Plan: find what stalls clears on 02tangle (a camp-group death loop? a target ban?).
  - Why: unseen sprint maps include large ones, and this extends Q28 (farming tempo).
- **Q31: turn-1 fuel on 100×100 maps, the concrete form of Q23.**
  - Evidence: q24 suspends each champion twice in rounds 1-2 on 01big-world and 02tangle (10 vs tb's 5 and 2). These are the only maps where q24's total fuelExh exceeds tb's (38/31, 45/32).
  - Plan: move `init_gold`'s reachability flood and the static caches into the draft (250M fuel) via draft memory.
  - Why: free opening turns on exactly the maps where q24 is weakest.
- **Q32: stall-map gold race.**
  - Evidence: on 00stampede (0-0 all game, no `no_tribute` flag) q24 lost both sides on gold (8,688 vs 9,152; 7,743 vs 9,835) with even clears. Stalled mode turns on only chip mode and the respawn fix, not gold valuation or Treasure emblems.
