## Testable hypotheses (structure-keyed)
1. **H-siege (W1).**
   - **Detect.** Siege = ≥ 2 enemy champions seen within 10 of our spawn region in the last 30 rounds, or ≥ 2 ally deaths within 10 of spawn in the last 150 rounds.
   - **Respond.** While besieged:
     - (a) A healed champion on spawn tiles waits there (they heal to full at our turn end) until ≥ 2 allies are on or next to spawn. Then all leave on the same turn toward the target whose path passes farthest from the last enemy sighting.
     - (b) A lone champion off spawn targets only groups within 6 of an ally.
   - **Validate.** Against a spawn-camping imitation that stacks Power on kill gold. The existing `mb_imit_uvt` died 40 times per game and does not reproduce the ladder: it must kite and only engage with a body advantage.
   - **Metrics.** Deaths within 8 of our spawn per game; kills given away per 1,000 rounds.
2. **H-burst (W2; refines V3L-1).**
   - **Change.**
     - (a) Read each visible enemy's burst: `e.focus()` × the modifier of its damage spells, from `e.abilities()[i].info()` or `Champion.design(type)`. Count a spell only if `is_ready()` is readable for enemies; otherwise assume it is ready. An enemy's threat is max(basic dps, burst/2), and its reach is attack range or spell range + 1.
     - (b) **One-shot rule:** never step within (spell range + 1) of an enemy whose ready burst is ≥ our HP, unless `gb_near ≥ GROUP_ADV` and the enemy is in our attack range.
     - (c) Use the same numbers in `recall_safe` and `group_balance`.
   - **Metric.** Deaths to Focus casters per game (packs, interlock, lost_world: 21, 19, 12 Stellar kills).
3. **H-scale (W3).**
   - **Change.** After the second Bruiser emblem, alternate Bruiser and Power shards of the main stat, so the stat per gold stays competitive. Vesper (+33% gold passive) buys Treasure on its main stat first. Keyed on champion type and emblem count, not map.
   - **Precondition.** Run Q28b/Q28d first; T25 (Jungle-first) was rejected.
   - **Metrics.** Gold at r1000, main stat at r1000, K/D after r1000.
4. **H-corridor (W4).**
   - **Change.**
     - (a) In `retreat_fast`, replace the Chebyshev `spawn_cd` with a walking-distance field to our spawn (static, built once like `sfields`). Order the keys (damage, camp margin, **walking spawn distance if enemies are within 4, else -enemy distance**), so fleeing champions head home along walkable routes instead of straight away from the enemy into dead ends.
     - (b) Add the ranges of sleeping camps with strength ≥ 200 to the camp-margin key and to `avoid` in the low-HP `step_toward(spawn)`.
   - **Metrics.** Share of deaths in dead-end tiles (tiles whose only exits lead back toward the enemy); camp deaths per game.
5. **H-altar-contest (W5; extends "Altar-camping death loop").**
   - **Change.** If our nearest altar has had enemy tributes or ≥ 2 enemy sightings in the last 100 rounds, carriers bank at ≥ 10 gems (not 30) and only with ≥ 1 ally within 4. Ally carriers gather and tribute together, and use locks on arrival (try_lock already places one on an enemy channel).
   - **Metrics.** Tributes at the contested altar; gems lost within 8 of an altar.
6. **H-buddy (W1 and W6).**
   - **Change.** Once ≥ 2 enemy champions have been seen in our half in the last 50 rounds, `choose_target` multiplies the score of groups more than 8 from every ally (by claim and position) by 0.4. This reverses claim spreading only under threat.
   - **Metrics.** Solo deaths per game; clears per 100 rounds under threat.
