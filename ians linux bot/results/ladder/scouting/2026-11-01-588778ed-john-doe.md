## Testable hypotheses (structure-keyed)
1. **H-tpaltar (W2).**
   - **Compute once, per champion** (dash or not), for each altar:
     - `walk` = walking steps from spawn to the ring;
     - `tp` = walking steps to the nearest launch tile within 3 of a ring tile, + 3 for the channel.
   - **Change.** If `walk - tp ≥ 12`:
     - (a) `shop()` buys a TP on every visit;
     - (b) `do_tribute` walks to the launch tile and calls `crystal.use(ring tile)` instead of `step_toward` along the long path;
     - (c) the same shortcut applies to `pick_altar`'s distance.
   - **Structure only.** The data: road (ring 3 tiles from the spawn tile; walk 22 or 120), great_wall (walk 56-61 vs TP 30 for non-dashers), shortcut (44 or 109 vs 19).
   - **Metrics.** First tribute round; tributes per game; gems lost per carrier death on maps where `walk - tp ≥ 12`.
2. **H-nodash (W2).**
   - **Change.** In `choose_target`, add the walking cost from the group to the champion's own best altar (non-dash distances) to `cost` when the champion carries ≥ 15 gems. Extends Q27d's capped delivery term, keyed on `has_dash`.
     - Alternatively, lower `BANK_ANY` for non-dashers whose altar walk exceeds 40.
   - **Metrics.** Non-dasher tributes per game; carried-gem-rounds above 30.
3. **H-treasure-vesper (W1).**
   - **Change.** Vesper Vale buys a Treasure emblem of its main stat first (5 shards, 150 gold), then Bruiser.
   - **Second arm.** After 2 Bruiser emblems, every champion alternates Bruiser and Power shards of its main stat. Keyed on champion type and emblem count. Distinct from rejected T25 (Jungle-first).
   - **Metrics.** Vesper gold at r500/r1000; team gold at r1000; K/D after r1000.
4. **H-route-memory (W4).**
   - **Change.** Record each ally carrier death (tile, round) in a small per-champion memory of deaths seen within vision, plus our own. When the path to the preferred altar passes within 4 of such a tile from the last 200 rounds:
     - pick another altar if one is within +15 steps;
     - otherwise bank only with an ally within 4, or after waiting for one.
   - **Metric.** Gems lost per game in corridors (tiles with ≤ 2 walkable neighbours across).
5. **H-powergap (W5).**
   - **Change.** When a visible enemy's `max_health()` × `strength()` (or its Focus burst) exceeds 2x our best champion's, flag "power gap" for 150 rounds:
     - bank at ≥ 10;
     - skip targets within 10 of where that enemy was last seen;
     - fight it only with `gb_near ≥ GROUP_ADV`.
   - **Metrics.** Deaths after r1000 against power-stacked comps; gem race r1000-1500.
6. **H-pocket (W3).** Fix the heal branch as in set 910a58b9 (recall or `go_to` when spawn is not in our walk component; guard on repeated failed `step_toward`).
   - **Metric.** Quiet champion-rounds on 01shortcut, both sides, locally.

**Opponent-specific notes.**
- This bot is slow early and fuel-starved on huge maps. Win early on big maps (islands), and pressure Prism's big bags.
- Expect losses on wall- or hole-split altar maps until H-tpaltar exists, and on long games until our economy scales.
