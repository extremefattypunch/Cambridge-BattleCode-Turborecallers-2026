## Testable hypotheses (structure-keyed; no map or team names)
1. **H-monster/power (W1).** *Extends queue Q28d (Monster + Strength-weighted Bruiser) and Q28b; new data point: Power stacking of the main stat.*
   - **Change.** After the first Bruiser emblem of the main stat, buy one Monster emblem of the main stat. Then alternate Power (main stat) and Bruiser (Health), instead of the current S:H 2:1 Bruiser split.
     - Keep the first Bruiser so early fight HP is unchanged; T25 (Jungle-first) lost fights.
     - Keyed only on champion type and our own item counts.
   - **Metrics.** Clears/100r per phase; main-stat value at r500/r1000; first-capture round of strength-60+ groups; K/D must hold.
   - **Test.** Full `--maps all` vs the champion, plus a gauntlet including Jungle/Monster/Power imitations.
2. **H-pair (W2).** *Queue #22 H-pair; this set adds geodes, broken_curve and great_wall as evidence.*
   - **Change.** In `choose_target`, when `cycles` is in (4, 6] and an ally within 8 tiles has claimed the same group (or is idle or at spawn), compute the model with both champions: `tdps = dps + ally_dps`, damage split by 2. Then commit both: hold the claim with an x1.3 bonus until the group is cleared or one partner's HP falls below 60%.
