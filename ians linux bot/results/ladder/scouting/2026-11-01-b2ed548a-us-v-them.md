## Testable hypotheses (structure-keyed)
1. **H-burst (W1; highest priority).** *This is T35 h_antifocus (105/140 vs `imit_uvt2`, against c2's 76; kept as a combo component, inside c5_combo). Its fuel-clean version is V3L-1a. The one addition proposed here is (a): `threats_fast().incoming`, which feeds the camp-danger check, is not in h_antifocus's list of changes.*
   - **Change.** Add a per-enemy spell term from its champion type and live Focus:
     - Stellar 2.0 + 1.4; Cassi 2.0; Marcella 2.0; Prism 1.5; Tavi 1.6; Ren 1.1 (+0.2×F retaliation); Ryth 1.0; HoR 1.5 (dash landing).
     - Spell reach is 4 + 1 for movement (+2 for a dash).
   - **Use it in three places:**
     - **(a) `threats_fast()` `incoming`:** when within reach, add `k×F` once if the enemy could cast this turn. Assume a spell is ready unless we saw it cast in the last 8 rounds.
     - **(b) `enemy_dps()`:** `strength + (sum k)×F/8`, keeping a burst term `k_max×F` for `their_ttk`.
     - **(c) `retreat_fast.danger()`:** reach = max(basic + 1, spell reach) and damage = max(Strength, k×F).
   - **Effect.** A champion then retreats beyond 5-6 tiles from a Power-Focus Stellar, and the fight model stops engaging when one spell kills.
   - **Validate** against `bots/imit_uvt2` (untracked; it imitates this comp, its Power-Focus builds and its bush wards) and the normal gate. Queue V3L-1d asks for a 3-pick version, which is where this set's disasters were (gold_rush, packs).
   - **Metrics.** Deaths to Focus casters per game; K/D vs `imit_uvt2`; gems at r500 on 31x31 maps.
2. **H-zone + regroup (W2).** *Queue #6 H-zone, #12 V2 spawn-pin escape, Q29 h_snowball. The respawn regroup in (b) is the new part.*
   - **(a) Zone decay.** Each champion keeps a per-group danger stamp (its own death or champion damage taken within 8 tiles of the group, or 2 or more enemies seen within 8 of it). Multiply the score by 0.3 for 150 rounds unless `group_balance` near ≥ `GROUP_ADV`.
   - **(b) Respawn regroup.** Count dead allies through the `ally_alive` map already kept in `update_alerts`. When a champion is on spawn with 2 or more allies dead, or an enemy was seen within 10 of our spawn in the last 20 rounds, it stays on spawn (full heal) until an ally is within 4 or 25 rounds pass. Then it walks out with the nearest ally, using `rally`.
   - **Metrics.** Deaths within 8 of our own spawn; death waves (2 or more deaths within 20 rounds) per game; captures per 100 rounds while an enemy team is on our half.
3. **H-bankfirst (W3).** *New. V3L-2c covers only low-HP carriers next to an altar.*
   - **Change.** In `play_turn`, when gems ≥ `BANK_ANY` (30) and `handle_enemies` would not find a `kill_now` target, run the tribute decision before `handle_enemies`. Retreat toward the chosen altar (rally = the altar ring) instead of the spawn.
   - **Also.** With 60 gems or more, never stand-and-trade beside awake camps.
   - **Metric.** Gems lost on death per game (treasure_box: 257); bags of 60 or more lost.
4. **H-reserve (W4).** *New.* As in the AI Coliseum report, H-reserve: with 3 or more allies already engaged and a local advantage, the best farmer leaves to farm. Pair it with H-pair (two champions commit to a 50-70-gem group whose pair model passes).
5. **H-goldcap (W5; queue #9).** Gold-only groups count only within about 12 path tiles, or when they are nearer our spawn than theirs (BFS margin).

**Opponent-specific notes.**
- **On small maps,** expect this bot to beat ours until W1-W2 are fixed. Its Stellar/Ren burst scales with kill gold, so the first 100 rounds decide the game: do not contest the centre against 4 bodies.
- **On big maps** it collapses on its own (fuel, camp deaths), and our farm wins.
- **Its weak spots:** Cassi + Issac die in pairs, Stellar is fragile when caught (treasure_box: 15 deaths), and there are no locks or wards outside bushes.
