## Testable hypotheses (structure-keyed)
1. **H-power (W1).** *Extends Q28d; the imitation is queue Q1 / V3L-5, which lists this team first.*
   - **Change.** Make the main stat's Power emblem the default after two Bruiser emblems: Power S for Vendetta and Vesper, Power F for Marcella, Power H for Ryth and HoR. Add one Monster emblem of the main stat early. Keep Bruiser H for the HP floor.
   - **Validation.** A local imitation of this opponent: a Vendetta / C&I / Ryth / Stellar / Varg draft with the build in §1 (Monster → Power → Durability → Jungle → Bruiser → Treasure → Power stacking). No such imitation exists yet; it is worth creating.
   - **Metrics.** Main stat at r1000; clears/100r per phase; boss-type gold at r1000.
2. **H-pair/H-team (W2).** *Queue #22 H-pair, Q26 / c4 (a dormant component in c5_combo) and H-commit.*
   - **Pair.** Two champions commit to a group the solo model refuses when the pair model passes: summed dps, damage split.
   - **Team.** For a monster group (strength 120 or more) worth 40 or more gems, muster 3 champions or more once the team's summed main stat passes a threshold. This generalises the queued T23/T30 team-clear.
   - **Metric.** Captures of groups with strength 120 or more, and gems from them.
3. **H-reserve (W3).** *New; not in the queue.*
   - **Change.** In `handle_enemies`, when 3 or more allies are already within `GROUP_R` of the same enemies and the local ratio is at least `GROUP_ADV`, the ally with the best farm score that is not adjacent to an enemy skips the fight (returns False) and farms. Bound it with shared memory: one byte holds the "fighters" count.
   - **Metrics.** Captures and tributes per 100 rounds while enemies are within 8 of our spawn; treasure-box-type games' gem totals.
4. **H-zone (W4, shared with the Electric Fog report).** *Queue #6; partly in Q29 h_snowball (dominant zones ×0.2).*
   - **Change.** Down-weight groups where we took champion damage or died in the last ~150 rounds by about 0.4, unless `group_balance` near-ratio ≥ `GROUP_ADV`.
   - **Also.** Don't chase a target into the 3 tiles around the enemy spawn region (their spawn heals).
   - **Metrics.** Deaths on the enemy half; champion damage taken with nothing dealt.
5. **H-burst (W5).** See the Us V Them report, H1. *T35 h_antifocus, kept as a combo component; its fuel-clean version is V3L-1a.* Model the ready-spell burst of Focus casters in `incoming`, in `enemy_dps` and in retreat danger.

**Opponent-specific notes.**
- **Their weak points:** Cassi & Issac (600/900 HP) die in pairs to our grouped fights. They lose many champions to camps (16 on reverse_spiral). They use no wards and no locks. Their score is concentrated in one carry.
- **What it would take to beat them:** match their item scaling, take the monster groups first, and hunt their carry. Without W1 and W2 fixed, expect losses on every map type: they won here on 30x30, 31x31, 35x35, 40x40 and 50x41.
