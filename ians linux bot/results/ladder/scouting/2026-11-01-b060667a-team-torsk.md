## 3. Their strategy vs ours

**Overall (2-3).** Their jungle comp beat us by strategy, not by our bugs. It farms the high-value Elk and Golem groups solo, with Monster + Jungle + Power builds, at about 1.7x our clear rate. Our solo safety model never lets a champion start those groups.
- Their burst comp lost to us by strategy: it does not farm elk groups and loses every grouped fight.
- Two execution errors of ours turned close jungle-comp games into losses:
  - **02uncharted_lands:** 45 gems carried into their spawn at r845-850.
  - **01starvation:** 18 gems lost at r1926 in a game decided by 16.

**Where ours was superior.**
- **Fighting (all 5 games).** Kills were 33-8 and 20-0 against the burst comp, and 16-10 on starvation. Our grouped fights (`group_balance`) win whenever we meet their champions in numbers.
- **Clear count on big, sparse maps:** starvation 74 to 15.
- **Farming elk groups against the burst comp:** interlock 847 elk gems to 44, corners 882 to 415. Mid elk groups are worth taking when the opponent is not there first. *Speculation:* the claim cascade let 2-3 of our champions share them.
- **Gold:** interlock 10,160 to 3,400; corners 9,616 to 3,870; starvation 4,267 to 2,360.

**What in our code causes the gaps.** All references are `bots/q24_combo/src/bot.py` line / `bots/c2_respawn/src/bot.py` line.
1. **No solo access to strong-camp groups:** the `choose_target` safety model, l.1394-1415 / 1413-1434.
   - It requires `dmg_solo = s*(hl/dps)*share*dmg_mult <= hp-150`, else hit-and-run with `budget = maxhp - s*dmg_mult*(G.rng+1) - 80` and `cycles = hl / (budget/(s*dmg_mult)*tdps) <= 4`.
   - **Elk x2 + GemCl x2** (s = 120, rng 4, hl ≈ 5,040): with Ryth at 1,700 max HP and dps ≈ 80, the budget is 1,020 and the per-cycle damage 680, so cycles ≈ 7.4 > 4. Rejected.
   - **Elk x4 field** (s = 240): the budget is `maxhp - 1280`, ≤ 0 below 1,280 max HP. Even at 2,000 HP, cycles ≈ 44. Never.
   - **Golem fields** (s = 120-240, hl 5,960-10,000+): rejected for the same reason.
   - Only an existing claim adds 55 dps per ally (`tdps = dps + c*55`). That is why we do take these groups when 2-3 champions happen to converge (interlock, corners) but not when one champion must start alone.
2. **Our build never buys camp passives.** `shop()` (l.929 / 948) calls `bruiser_item()` (l.973 / 992), which returns a Bruiser shard for every champion in `BRUISER_PLAN`, and all five of our picks are in it.
   - So the Jungle / Monster / Power branch of `shop()` is unreachable for our draft. `JUNGLE_FIRST = False` (l.126 / 128).
   - The safety model does use Jungle (`dmg_mult = 0.7`), but we never own one.
3. **Banking ignores where the carrier is going.** `bank_now` / `bank_detour` (l.1591 / 1610, l.1583 / 1602) use only bag size and distance thresholds: `BANK_ANY = 30`; 15 within a detour of 8; 5 within a detour of 3.
   - `threatened` (l.1524 / 1543) only halves them after a sighting within 14 in the last 40 rounds, an ally death in the last 100, or HP below 60%.
   - Nothing stops a 19-26 gem carrier from walking into the enemy half or spawn (uncharted r823-850).
4. **Enemies in sight pre-empt banking.** In `play_turn`, `handle_enemies` (l.2515 / v4 `play_turn_main`) runs before the tribute decision (l.2550-2577), and its retreat goes toward allies or the spawn, not toward an altar.
   - A carrier a few steps from a usable altar never chooses to channel through damage (starvation r1897-1926, *speculation* on the exact turns). Related queue item: V3L-2c.
5. **Fragile opponents are where our fight rules pay off.** `group_balance` (l.1917 / 1936) plus focus on channellers and Cassi/Issac linked deaths gave double kills at interlock r1364 and corners r312-414. Keep this.
