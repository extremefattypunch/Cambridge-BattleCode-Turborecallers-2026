## Testable hypotheses (structure-keyed; no map or team names)
1. **H-path-retreat (W1, highest priority).**
   - **Change.** Build one static walking-distance field from our spawn at init: `DistanceField.compute(walk_block, spawn)`. Use it for `retreat_fast`'s last key instead of `spawn_cd`.
     - During a regroup, use the walking distance to the rally ally only within a small radius; otherwise fall back to the spawn field.
     - When an enemy is within 6, drop any candidate tile whose walking distance to spawn is ≥ 1.6x its Chebyshev distance **and** greater than the current tile's walking distance (no stepping deeper into a pocket).
   - **Metrics.**
     - deaths on tiles with walking/Chebyshev distance to spawn > 1.6;
     - deaths per encounter with ≥ 3 enemies;
     - local mechanism check on 01hooks A/B against a hunting imitation.
   - **Gate.** Full `--maps all`. Retreat runs every fight, so check fight K/D on open maps too.
2. **H-stack-muster (W2).**
   - **Change.** When a champion sees ≥ 3 enemy champions within 5 of each other, it writes a "stack" flag (round + position, one shared-memory byte). For the next 30 rounds, every ally within 25 walking tiles that is not already in a fight with `adv` walks, by the H1 field, to the tile on the path from spawn toward the stack that is 3-4 steps outside spawn. It engages only when `group_balance` near-ratio ≥ 1.0. Allies on spawn wait there, since spawn heals at turn end.
   - **Metrics.** Multi-death fight record against grouped enemies; our deaths in our own half.
3. **H-retarget-hysteresis (W4).**
   - **Change.** Once a status-2 target is chosen, keep it, and skip the `G.ready > rnd + d` exclusion for the current target, until we reach its ring or another target scores ≥ 1.5x.
   - **Combine** with the idle watchdog from the 008a189a/ac72833b reports: 80 rounds with no activity-counter change → ban the target for 150 rounds.
   - **Metric.** `tools/freeze_metric.py` on the local 02tangle (known G25↔G27 trace) and the full pool.
4. **H-vesper-treasure (W3).**
   - **Change.** Vesper's first emblem becomes Treasure (passive +33% → +43%), even when tributes are possible; Bruiser after.
   - **Variant.** Prospector second on maps where 0-gem cluster groups hold ≥ 40% of map gold (a structural map feature).
   - **Metrics.** Gold at r500 and r1000, emblems at r1000, K/D.
5. **H-goldcap (W5/W6, shared).** Groups whose gem value is < 25% of their total value are scored only within about 15 walking tiles of the champion, the altar or our spawn, unless `gold_play()` is on.
   - **Metrics.** Walking rounds per gem; gems at r250.
