## 3. Which strategy was superior, and what we learn
- **Overall (4-1):** our strategy was superior over the set, being better on four maps (00split, 01islands, 01broken_curve narrowly, and 01yellow_brick_road until r530) and worse only on 02uncharted_lands, but the results of the two decisive games came from bugs rather than strategy:
  - their team-wide freeze gave us uncharted lands (they led 783-713 at r947);
  - our capture-standoff freeze gave them yellow brick road, which we were leading 82-19 in gems and 3,443-628 in gold.

- **Lessons:**
  1. **A capture must have an exit.** Our capture branch waits forever. On a long group (a 30-tile row here) one idle enemy anywhere on its region blocks the capture for good.
     - This standoff was documented locally on this exact map and side (`bots/c2_respawn/NOTES.md`, "contested-capture standoff", 1,650 rounds in the 15-4 game vs q24_combo; STRATEGIES "c2 H3").
     - It was left unfixed and has now cost a ladder game outright.
  2. **One champion per capture.** Allies don't speed a capture up (CAPTURE_TIME 5, rewards split). Yet a 600-gold, 0-gem group pulled all five of our champions, against one or two of theirs. Their bot hit the same standoff and still won, because three of its champions kept farming.
  3. **Long idle stretches recur.**
     - The cases: broken curve Vesper r541-702 and Marcella r547-733; uncharted Marcella r1030-1216; islands Ryth r216-375.
     - One generic watchdog (no metric change for N rounds: drop the target, heal, retarget) would catch this class, the standoff included.
  4. **On gem-cluster maps our gold falls behind.** Uncharted: 909 vs 2,333 gold at r500. The group rule then (correctly) avoids every fight while the richer opponent picks us off. Gold has to be valued more when we are behind on items.
  5. **The 4-1 summarises the strategies fairly, but the wrong games went each way.** We were better on four maps and worse on uncharted, yet uncharted is the game we won (their freeze) and yellow brick road the one we lost (our freeze). What carries over:
     - against a bug-free bot with this build (Treasure first, own-half farming), we lose maps where our income is gem clusters with little gold;
     - against any bot, we lose maps with a long capture region until W1 and W2 are fixed.
- **Where our approach was better (keep):**
  - **Opening tempo.** We were ahead at r250 in all 5 games, and our first tribute was 29-236 rounds earlier in every game.
  - **Carrier punishment and small banking.** Split: 12 vs 311 gems lost on death; on split and yellow brick road we lost 20 gems on death in total.
  - **Grouped early fights.** Yellow brick road 16-3 by r524, all between the middle and their spawn; `group_balance` / `GROUP_ADV` and `focus_bias` work against this kind of bot.
  - **Map-specific mobility and objectives.** TP crystals and multi-champion captures of big centre groups (islands 464 gems, which they never took); gem-dense targets on broken curve.
